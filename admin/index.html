How editing works

Open any project on the map and press the pencil in its top bar. Change what you like and press Save. The site updates in a few minutes; until then the pencil shows a blue dot.

Your edits are permanent: the daily rebuild never overwrites them. The one exception is the stage, which the city's permit records can still move forward (never back, and never off Stalled). Each edit is saved as its own file in data/edits/ on GitHub, so there's a full history.
